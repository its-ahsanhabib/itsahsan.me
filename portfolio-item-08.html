VOICE CONTROLLED HOME AUTOMATION

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Google Home is a smart speaker developed by Google. Google Home enables users to speak voice commands to interact with services through the Home’s intelligent personal assistant called Google Assistant. Google home has an AI-API developed by google. It can trigger Smart home appliance and implement the daily used task by the user voice command as it can turn on or off your light, fan, AC, play music, search Web.

It can play music, can answer any question, “That is available in internet” and can control appliance, but google do not developing the controlling peripherals for the smart home appliance other 3rd party company like “Philips hue” “Belkin Wemo” are making those things. However, world largest home automation company like “NEST” “Honeywell” “TP-Link” are also coming on this AI or Home Assistant service. As mention, Amazon also developed “Amazon Echo” and Apple has claimed that they have a device that called “HomePod” which will be available in this year December. Coming tomorrow will be the time of home assistant.
